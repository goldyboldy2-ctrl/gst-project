About MyGSTIndia

I'm Manoj, and I built MyGSTIndia because I kept seeing GST compliance treated as something only accountants could untangle — when in reality, most small business owners just need a straight answer to a specific number: how much interest, how much penalty, whether their ITC is valid, what rate applies to their HSN code.

I come from a finance background, not a coding one — this site is built and maintained by me directly, calculator by calculator, based on actual GST provisions and CBIC notifications. I'm not a CA or a tax lawyer, and I say that deliberately: every tool here is built to be a clear reference point, not a substitute for professional advice on your specific situation.

I run this as a long-term project, updating it as GST rules change — not a one-time build. If you spot something outdated or wrong, get in touch — I read and act on that feedback myself.
